# The problem we are solving

## The nonprofit
<!-- Name, and what they do in one sentence. -->
Harbour Hosuing Alliance is an emergency shelter network with six sehlters which finds open beds for people while taking minimal information and ensuring every person is lead to an open bed.

## Who will use what we build
<!-- The specific person: role, how comfortable they are with technology, what device they use. -->
We are solving for the staff members who lead people to the housing. Those being the caseworkers and front desk workers, they have minor tech comfort, and it needs to support desktop/laptop and mobile use, via a website.

## The one problem
<!-- One sentence, in their words. What do they do today, and what does it cost them? -->
Caseworkers spend unncessary time finding a suitable option for people, there is no updated information by front of house to caseworker and vice-versa costing them the ability to get people in beds.

## The demo story
<!-- Three steps a judge will watch: someone opens the app, does one thing, and gets a result that wasn't possible before. -->
1. Caseworker opens app, fills in information which becomes filters and showcases suitable option for client. Front desk through 2 simple buttons log check in/out
2. Can view recent booking information and web decision on if booking is available (a) or unavailable (b). if a. given option in website for direct reservation, if b. contact route is available for confirmation
3. Beds are never turned down unncessarily.
